NIST UNIVERSITY

Creating knowledge. Creating opportunities.

NIST University, located at Institute Park, Berhampur, Odisha, was established in 1996. The university is situated in the green hills of Pallur and has developed a strong academic and research environment.

The campus brings together classrooms, laboratories, research facilities, library, residential facilities, sports and other student-centric infrastructure.
